a letter, quietly written

for you, love.

something i've been wanting to say.

↓
i don't really know how to begin this. there are so many things i want to say, and somehow, "i love you" doesn't feel enough for what i want you to understand tonight. because tonight, i don't just want to tell you that i love you. i want to tell you that i see you. not just the girl i fell in love with. but the person behind her. the person you were before me. the person you became while i was getting to know you. and the person you're still becoming today.
sometimes i think about the things you've told me about your childhood. little things you've mentioned in conversations that you probably didn't think much about. going to school by yourself when you were still in elementary. riding public transportation by yourself. sometimes walking home after school even though your home was far. and every time i remember those things, i imagine how young you were. you were just a kid. and yet, you were already learning how to find your way home. the heat. the distance. the tiredness. the traffic. carrying your things. just trying to get home after a long day. ordinary difficulties, maybe. things you probably don't think are a big deal. but i think about that kid a lot. and i know your parents have worked abroad since you were young. i won't pretend to know exactly what that was like for you. i won't pretend to know every feeling you had growing up. that's your story, and only you truly know what it felt like. but knowing even a small part of it makes me appreciate the person you've become even more.
and love, i'm proud of you. not because you're strong in the way people usually describe strength. i don't mean how much you can carry. i don't mean how much you can endure without complaining. i mean something much quieter. you learned how to move forward. you learned how to guide yourself. you learned your own limits. you learned when to continue and when to stop. you learned how to make decisions for yourself. and you learned how to keep going even when there wasn't always someone physically beside you to guide every step.
because sometimes i don't think you realize how much i see in you. i see your effort. the time you give. the money you spend. the little things you remember. the things you do even when nobody asks you to. the moments when you could have simply said, "i'm tired," but you still tried. your patience. your presence. the way you make time for me. maybe i don't always say it at the exact moment you do them. maybe sometimes i get too caught up in my own thoughts, or school, or whatever is happening around me. but please know that i notice.

i notice you.

and i appreciate you.
and i hope you know that you're not just some random girl who happened to enter my life. you're not someone i simply met and started dating. you're my partner. you're someone whose life i genuinely care about. someone whose past makes me want to understand her more. someone whose present makes me proud. and someone whose future i want to witness.
sometimes i wish i could go back. not to change anything. not to make her life easier. not because i think she needed someone to save her. just to meet her. just for a moment. to see the little girl who was already learning how to find her own way. and tell her: "you're going to be okay." "keep going." "you're going to grow into someone amazing." "and one day, someone is going to be incredibly proud of you."

and that someone is me.

i don't expect you to be perfect. i don't expect you to be strong every day. you are allowed to rest. you are allowed to struggle. you are allowed to have days where you don't know what you're doing. and even then, you're still you. still the girl i love. still my partner. i see your past. i see your present. and i want to see your future. not because i expect you to be perfect. not because i expect you to always be strong. but because i genuinely want to be there while you continue becoming yourself.
thank you for your time. thank you for your effort. thank you for your patience. thank you for your little sacrifices. thank you for the things you give. thank you for the memories. thank you for trusting me with pieces of your life. and most of all... thank you for being you. i hope you never forget that i see you. i appreciate you. and i'm proud of you. always.
before you go... there's one more thing i want you to see. you.

— your stan 🍓💗
